# Introduction to GRC

## What is GRC?

Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) is a structured approach that helps organizations align business objectives with cybersecurity, manage risks effectively, and comply with applicable laws, regulations, and industry standards.

The three core components of GRC are:

### Governance
Governance establishes the policies, processes, and decision-making structure that guide an organization toward achieving its business and security objectives.

### Risk Management
Risk Management involves identifying, assessing, evaluating, and treating risks that could impact an organization's assets, operations, or reputation.

### Compliance
Compliance ensures that an organization adheres to legal, regulatory, contractual, and internal policy requirements.

---

## Why GRC is Important

GRC helps organizations:

- Improve decision-making
- Protect business assets and sensitive information
- Reduce cybersecurity and business risks
- Meet legal and regulatory requirements
- Build trust with customers and stakeholders
- Support business continuity

## Key Takeaways

After completing this topic, I understand that:

- GRC integrates Governance, Risk, and Compliance into one strategy.
- Governance provides direction and accountability.

- Compliance ensures adherence to applicable laws, regulations, and standards.
- A strong GRC program supports business resilience and long-term success.
